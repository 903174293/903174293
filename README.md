## About Me

**Systems Engineer · Architect · Full-Stack Engineer**

I build and own systems end to end — infrastructure, service design, APIs, and the interface on top of them.

Day to day I work mainly in **Go, Java and C++**, and make architecture decisions with one test in mind: does this still hold when traffic doubles, the team changes, and the system has to live for another two years.

Outside of work I maintain **[Novordoc](https://github.com/Novordoc)** — a knowledge organization that turns scattered technical material into something structured, searchable and reusable.

Also exploring **Godot game development**, **Solidity**, and **compute infrastructure** — breadth on purpose, depth where it matters.
